About Bihar Cooperative Bank

A member-owned cooperative bank serving Bihar for over seven decades (fictional).

Our story

Bihar Cooperative Bank Ltd. was founded in 1952 by a group of farmer cooperatives in Patna to give rural families access to fair credit. Today we serve more than 12 lakh members through 74 branches, banking correspondents and digital channels.

As a cooperative, our profits go back to our members as better rates, lower charges and community programmes for agriculture, women's self-help groups and small enterprises.

Our values

  • Member first: every product is designed around the needs of the people who own the bank.
  • Trust and transparency: clear pricing, no hidden charges.
  • Privacy by design: we collect only the data we need and ask before using it for anything optional.
  • Financial inclusion: banking in every block of Bihar.
