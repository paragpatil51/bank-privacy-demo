Privacy Policy & Privacy Notice

How Bihar Cooperative Bank Ltd. (fictional) collects, uses and protects your personal data.

This policy belongs to a fictional bank built for a technical demo. It is modelled on India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) but is not legal advice.

1. Who we are

Bihar Cooperative Bank Ltd. (“the Bank”, “we”) is the Data Fiduciary for personal data collected through this website. Our consent records are managed using a dedicated consent management platform.

2. What personal data we collect

  • Identity and contact details: full name, email address, mobile number, date of birth.
  • Application details: account type and preferred branch.
  • Consent records: the choices you make, the version of this notice you saw and when you made them.
  • Website data: strictly necessary cookies, and analytics or marketing cookies only if you allow them (see our Cookie Policy).

We do not collect Aadhaar, PAN or account numbers through this website. KYC documents are verified separately.

Optional purposes are never pre-selected and are never a condition for opening an account.

4. Who we share data with

  • Regulators, law-enforcement agencies and KYC registries where the law requires it.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf (for example IT hosting and SMS providers) under contracts that require confidentiality and security.
  • Our insurance partner, only if you give consent for that purpose.

5. How long we keep your data

Account and KYC records are retained for the period required by banking and anti-money-laundering rules (typically 5 years after the relationship ends). Data used for optional purposes is deleted or no longer used for that purpose once you withdraw consent. Consent records themselves are kept as proof of your choices.

6. How we protect your data

Personal data is encrypted in our database, access is limited to authorised staff and every access to full customer details is logged. Our website uses HTTPS and strict security controls.

7. Your rights

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed.
  • Correct, complete or update your data.
  • Request erasure of data that is no longer needed, subject to legal retention.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, using your consent management link. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with our Data Protection Officer, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.
