Frequently asked questions

What do I need to open an account online?
Your full name, email, mobile number and date of birth. KYC documents are verified at a branch or through video KYC after you apply. Online opening is available to applicants aged 18 and above.
Do I have to agree to marketing to open an account?
No. Marketing communications and sharing with our insurance partner are optional and switched off by default. Your account is opened the same way whatever you choose.
How do I change or withdraw my consent?
Use the consent management link shown after you apply (in production this is also emailed to you), or contact our Data Protection Officer. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent and takes effect immediately.
How do I change my cookie choices?
Click “Cookie settings” in the footer or the cookie icon at the bottom-left of any page. Optional cookies are only used after you allow them.
How do I raise a grievance?
Contact customer care first. If you are not satisfied within 7 working days, write to our Grievance Officer (see Contact us). You may also approach the RBI Ombudsman or the Data Protection Board of India (illustrative).
