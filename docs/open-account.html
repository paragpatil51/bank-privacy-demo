Privacy notice: account opening (version 2026.10, effective 2026-10-01)

Who is collecting your data: Bihar Cooperative Bank Ltd. (fictional), Fraser Road, Patna, as Data Fiduciary.

What we collect: your full name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, account type and preferred branch.

Why we collect it

    Sharing

    With regulators and KYC registries where the law requires; with IT and SMS service providers acting for us; and with our insurance partner only if you agree.

    Retention

    Application and KYC data are kept as long as banking law requires. Data for optional purposes stops being used when you withdraw consent.

    Your rights

    You can access, correct and erase your data, withdraw any consent at any time (as easily as giving it), nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance with our Data Protection Officer (dpo@bcb-demo.example) and then the Data Protection Board of India.

    Required to process your application

    This is an acknowledgement, not a marketing consent. We need this processing to open the account you asked for and to meet legal obligations.

    Optional: your choice

    You can open your account without any of these. They are off unless you switch them on, and you can change them at any time.
