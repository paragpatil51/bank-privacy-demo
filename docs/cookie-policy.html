What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Some are essential for a website to work; others help us measure use of the site or show relevant advertising.

Categories we use

  • Strictly necessary: always on; needed for security and to remember your cookie choice.
  • Analytics (optional): off until you allow it. Measures how the site is used.
  • Marketing (optional): off until you allow it. Measures campaigns and personalises offers.

How we honour your choice

  • Optional scripts are not loaded at all until you consent; showing the banner is not treated as consent.
  • “Reject optional” is as easy to choose as “Accept all”.
  • Your choice is recorded with a consent ID, a timestamp and the policy version (v1.0). If we change this policy, we ask you again.
  • Your choice expires after 180 days and we ask again.
  • If you switch a category off, we delete its cookies and reload the page so its scripts stop.
